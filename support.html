Common questions

How do I get more retries?
You get free retries every day. Outside the EU you can also watch a short ad for another try. Replaying a level you have already won, to get a better medal, is always free.

What are the medals?
Finish a level fast for gold, a bit slower for silver, any time for bronze. The clock only runs while you play: it stops for menus and tutorials.

How do I change my privacy choices?
Open the menu (three lines, top right) and tap "Privacy choices". This option appears where the law asks us to offer it.

Will I lose my progress if I uninstall?
Yes. Your progress is saved only on your phone, so uninstalling the game deletes it.
