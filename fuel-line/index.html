Fuel Line

A car jam puzzle for iPhone and Android.

Tap cars out of a packed lot and line them up at the pump in the right order, before the queue fills up. Each fuel truck carries one colour, so plan which cars go first.

300 levels, with new twists along the way: buses and coaches, frozen cars, mystery cars, coloured gates, switch arrows, locked cars and cars that change colour.
