Jelly Nap

A cosy jelly puzzle for iPhone.

A sleepy animal is fast asleep on a board on top of a tower of jelly. Pop groups of matching jelly to bring it down to its cushion, but keep the board level and don't drop it too far, or it wakes up!

200 levels, ten sleepy friends and ten cosy rooms, with new twists along the way: stones, bubbles, frozen jelly, alarm clocks, little ones to bring down too, and soft marshmallow.
