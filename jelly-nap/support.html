Common questions

How do I get more retries?
You get 3 free retries every day, and the first 15 levels are always free to retry. After that you can watch a short ad or use coins for another try.

Why did it wake up?
A sleeper wakes if one pop drops it more than 2 blocks at once. Watch the Wake meter at the top: green is deep sleep, red means a wake-up is close. Press and hold a group to see what a pop would do before you let go.

Why did it slide off?
If one end of the board hangs 2 blocks lower than the other, the board tips. Leaning by 1 block is fine.

How do I change my privacy choices?
Open the menu (three lines, top right) and tap "Privacy choices". This option appears where the law asks us to offer it.

Will I lose my progress if I uninstall?
Yes. Your progress is saved only on your phone, so uninstalling the game deletes it.
