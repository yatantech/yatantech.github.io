Fuse Turn

A fiery rope puzzle for iPhone and Android.

A match lights a rope, and the fire runs along it, splitting at every junction. Turn the rope pieces to send it to the rockets, and keep it away from the TNT crate that Biscuit the cat is sleeping on.

200 levels with new twists along the way: ropes that are already burning, slow wet rope, fast gunpowder, tap limits, two matches, bridges, linked pieces and rockets that must launch in order.
