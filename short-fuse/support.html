Common questions

How do I get more retries?
You get 3 free retries every day, and the first 15 levels are always free to retry. After that you can watch a short ad or use coins for another try.

What is "Rewind the fire"?
After a fail you can rewind once with a short ad or coins (and a second time with coins). The fire goes back to the start, the pieces you turned stay turned, there is no tap limit, and the fire waits for "Light it!".

Why did it go boom?
Fire spreads down every connected rope at once, so a piece pointing the wrong way at a junction can send it to the TNT. You can still turn pieces the fire has not reached yet, even while it burns.

How do I change my privacy choices?
Open the pause menu (top right) and tap "Privacy". The choices appear where the law asks us to offer them.

Will I lose my progress if I uninstall?
Yes. Your progress is saved only on your phone, so uninstalling the game deletes it.
